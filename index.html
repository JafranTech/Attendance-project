FAQ

Quick answers

Is my attendance data stored on a server? +
No. All your data is stored locally on your device using LocalStorage. There is no cloud sync, no data collection, and no servers involved. Your data is entirely private.
Can I use it without installing it as an app? +
Yes! AttendEase runs in any mobile or desktop browser at students-erp.vercel.app. You can optionally install it as a PWA from your browser for a faster, app-like experience with offline support.
How does it handle lab sessions? +
Long lab blocks are automatically split into multiple individual periods. So a 3-hour lab counts as 3 attendance periods — exactly like your college does it.
What happens if I clear my browser data? +
Since data is stored in LocalStorage, clearing your browser data will remove your attendance records. We recommend using the Elite plan's backup feature to keep a copy safe.
Is the Pro plan really just ₹10? +
Yes — ₹10 per month for the Pro plan, and ₹29 for the entire semester with the Elite plan. Payments are handled securely through Razorpay. No auto-renewal without your consent.
